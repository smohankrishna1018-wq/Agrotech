Chilli Cultivation Guide

Complete guide for successful chilli farming.

Overview

Chilli is an important spice crop grown throughout India. It is used for fresh consumption, drying, powder production, and export.

Suitable Soil

  • Well Drained Sandy Loam Soil
  • Loamy Soil
  • pH 6.0 - 7.0

Climate

Temperature 20°C - 30°C
Rainfall 600 - 800 mm
Growing Duration 120 - 180 Days

Water Requirement

Chilli requires moderate irrigation. Avoid excess watering to prevent fungal diseases.

Medium Water Requirement

Fertilizer Schedule

Stage Fertilizer
Land Preparation Farm Yard Manure + DAP
30 Days Urea
Flowering NPK + Micronutrients
Fruit Development Potash

Common Diseases

  • Leaf Curl Virus
  • Anthracnose
  • Powdery Mildew
  • Damping Off

Harvesting

Harvest green chillies when they reach full size. For dry chillies, allow fruits to ripen completely before harvesting.

Expected Yield

25 - 40 Quintals / Acre

Estimated Profit

Expense ₹50,000 - ₹80,000 / Acre
Income ₹1,20,000 - ₹2,50,000 / Acre
Estimated Profit ₹70,000 - ₹1,70,000 / Acre

Smart Farming Tips

  • 🌶 Use certified hybrid seeds.
  • 💧 Install drip irrigation for better water efficiency.
  • 🌱 Apply mulch to conserve soil moisture.
  • 🦠 Monitor plants regularly for pests and diseases.
  • ☀ Harvest during cool morning hours for better quality.
