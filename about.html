About AgroSense

Smart Agriculture Management Platform

Our Mission

AgroSense is a smart agriculture platform designed to help farmers make better decisions using modern technology. The system combines weather information, crop guidance, irrigation planning, and disease detection in a single easy-to-use website.

Key Features

Weather Forecast

Monitor weather conditions and rainfall forecasts.

Smart Irrigation

Calculate crop water requirements and irrigation schedules.

Crop Guide

Detailed cultivation information for major crops.

Disease Detection

Upload crop images for AI-assisted disease analysis.
