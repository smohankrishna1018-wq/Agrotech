Paddy Cultivation Guide

Complete information for successful rice farming.

Overview

Paddy (Rice) is one of India's most important food crops. It grows best in warm climates with sufficient water.

Suitable Soil

  • Clay Soil
  • Clay Loam Soil
  • Rich Organic Matter

Climate

Temperature 20°C - 35°C
Rainfall 1000 - 2000 mm
Season Kharif

Water Requirement

Requires approximately 1200–1500 mm of water during the growing season.

High Water Requirement

Fertilizer Schedule

Stage Fertilizer
Basal DAP + FYM
Tillering Urea
Panicle Initiation Potash

Common Diseases

  • Rice Blast
  • Brown Spot
  • Sheath Blight
  • Bacterial Leaf Blight

Harvesting

Harvest after 120–150 days when 80–85% grains become golden yellow.

Estimated Yield

25–35 Quintals / Acre

Profit Estimate

Expense ₹30,000–45,000 / Acre
Income ₹60,000–90,000 / Acre
Estimated Profit ₹25,000–45,000 / Acre

Smart Tips

  • 🌱 Use certified seeds.
  • 💧 Maintain proper water depth.
  • 🌾 Apply fertilizer at recommended stages.
  • 🦠 Monitor diseases regularly.
  • 🚜 Harvest at the correct maturity stage.
