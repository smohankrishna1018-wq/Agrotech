Tomato Cultivation Guide

Complete guide for profitable tomato farming.

Overview

Tomato is one of the most widely cultivated vegetable crops. It has high market demand and is suitable for both open-field and protected cultivation.

Suitable Soil

  • Well-drained Loamy Soil
  • Sandy Loam Soil
  • pH 6.0 – 7.5

Climate

Temperature 18°C – 30°C
Rainfall 400 – 600 mm
Growing Duration 90 – 120 Days

Water Requirement

Tomato requires moderate irrigation. Avoid waterlogging to reduce disease risk.

Medium Water Requirement

Fertilizer Schedule

Stage Fertilizer
Before Planting Compost + DAP
30 Days NPK
Flowering Potash + Micronutrients
Fruit Development Calcium + Potassium

Common Diseases

  • Early Blight
  • Late Blight
  • Bacterial Wilt
  • Leaf Curl Virus

Harvesting

Harvest tomatoes when fruits are firm and fully colored according to market requirements.

Expected Yield

20–35 Tons / Acre

Estimated Profit

Expense ₹60,000–90,000 / Acre
Income ₹1,20,000–2,00,000 / Acre
Estimated Profit ₹60,000–1,10,000 / Acre

Smart Farming Tips

  • 🍅 Use disease-resistant hybrid varieties.
  • 💧 Prefer drip irrigation.
  • 🌱 Support plants with staking.
  • 🦠 Monitor for pests and fungal diseases regularly.
  • 🌞 Harvest during cool morning hours for better shelf life.
