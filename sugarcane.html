Sugarcane Cultivation Guide

Complete guide for high-yield sugarcane farming.

Overview

Sugarcane is a long-duration commercial crop used for sugar, jaggery, ethanol, and biofuel production.

Suitable Soil

  • Deep Loamy Soil
  • Clay Loam Soil
  • Well Drained Soil

Climate

Temperature 20°C – 35°C
Rainfall 1200–1800 mm
Growing Period 10–12 Months

Water Requirement

Sugarcane requires approximately 1500–2500 mm of water during its growth period.

Very High Water Requirement

Fertilizer Schedule

Stage Fertilizer
Planting FYM + DAP
45 Days Urea
90 Days Potash
120 Days NPK Mixture

Common Diseases

  • Red Rot
  • Smut
  • Wilt Disease
  • Leaf Scald

Harvesting

Harvest sugarcane after 10–12 months when canes become mature and sweet.

Expected Yield

35–50 Tons / Acre

Estimated Profit

Expense ₹50,000–70,000 / Acre
Income ₹90,000–1,40,000 / Acre
Estimated Profit ₹40,000–70,000 / Acre

Smart Farming Tips

  • 💧 Adopt drip irrigation to save water.
  • 🌱 Use healthy seed canes.
  • 🌾 Apply fertilizer in split doses.
  • 🚜 Remove weeds regularly.
  • 🦠 Monitor crops for early disease detection.
